FEATURED RESEARCH / AI

AI-assisted screening through multimodal interaction.

My research interests focus on intelligent systems that combine multiple forms of human input to create more adaptive, accessible and meaningful digital experiences.

AI Driven Autism Spectrum Disorder Screening Tool


During an AI research internship at the Centre of Neuroinformatics, I worked on the research and implementation of an AI-driven system designed to support preliminary Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) screening.

The system brings together speech, text and behavioural information to support a multimodal assessment process. It incorporates adaptive questioning, AI-based analysis and structured report generation to make the screening experience more responsive to individual inputs.

Research and technical contributions

The implementation explores multimodal language-model analysis, adaptive assessment and the generation of structured reports informed by recognised diagnostic frameworks, including DSM-5 and ICD-11.


Explainability techniques such as SHAP and LIME were also incorporated to support the interpretation of model outputs. The system is intended as a screening tool and does not replace assessment or diagnosis by clinical professionals.


From Research to intellectual property

The work contributed to a published patent, bringing together AI research, system implementation and the design of an interactive assessment experience.
